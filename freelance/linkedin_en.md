## About

I'm a designer by training, and I got tired of handing over mockups and hoping they would survive development. So now I do both: I design the product and I build it, using AI tools for the code.

For you this means one person answers for how it looks and how it works. You get a live link, not a design file and a to-do list for someone else.

What I take on:

- Websites and landing pages, from the first sketch to a published page
- UX/UI and product design for web and mobile apps: user flows, wireframes, clickable prototypes, final screens
- Small web apps and AI-powered tools: chat assistants, internal dashboards, forms that do something useful with the data
- Telegram bots for bookings, orders, support and notifications
- Automation of routine work: spreadsheets, reports, connecting services to each other
- Pitch decks and presentations

I'm at the start of my freelance path, so I keep things simple: small, clearly scoped projects, a fixed price, and something you can click on within the first few days. AI writes a large part of the code. I decide what gets built, test it, and answer for the result. For bigger jobs I bring in a small team I trust.

If you have an idea and no clear picture of where to start, message me. I'll tell you plainly what it would take.
